## Synthesis

The evidence does not support another broad visual program. It supports one serialized selector-local accessibility slice spanning two presentation owners under a single measurable contract. Copy/name and field-label findings remain named backlog rather than silently inflating the implementation.

Recommended successor result: `ACCESSIBILITY_NECESSITY_ONLY`.
