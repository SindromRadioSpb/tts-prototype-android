## Cross-cutting blockers before external mentor-led positioning

1. P8.6 operational rollout evidence (token rotation, firewall, purge, kill-switch, rollback with MA events, delete/export completeness).
2. Independently validated transfer metric and grader rubric.
3. Public consent/lifecycle copy aligned with actual cloud/Telegram behavior.
4. Explicit canon transition/rollback proof for more than owner profile.
5. Product identity test proving users understand the reading-led promise and Studio/Room boundary.

## Major risks

Cold-start mentor without evidence; curation gap; Home overload; notification fatigue; premature speaking/writing claims; cost optimizing for messages; sparse-evidence misconception labels; Anki/interoperability edge debt; mixing corpus difficulty with learner ability.

## Strategic opportunities

Hebrew-specific form/lemma/context evidence; contextual transfer metric; gradual scaffolding withdrawal; a trusted curated progression through real Hebrew; mentor recommendations that are inspectable and reversible.

## Facts / assumptions / unresolved / sources

**FACT:** many technical gates already exist and pass owner-live tests. **INFERENCE:** the remaining highest risks are external validity and product clarity. **PROPOSAL:** treat every BLOCKER above as a launch gate and every MAJOR as a measured acceptance criterion. **ASSUMPTION:** owner-live paths are representative enough for operational debugging, but not outcome validation. **UNRESOLVED:** findings remain gates, not implied approval.
