# 03 — Strategy A: Reading Intelligence

**Status:** PROPOSAL · **Source commit:** `b426b1b7a91abcb4afb8fde7f0e34c042a9bc0d6` · **Date:** 2026-07-11

## Canvas

- **Target/problem:** A2–B2 independent learner; authentic Hebrew is either incomprehensible or destroyed by tool-switching.
- **Promise:** read a real text with only the support needed, then carry its difficult language into durable, contextual memory.
- **First value:** one sentence becomes readable in under three minutes without losing the original text.
- **Repeat loop:** choose/import → read → tap only when blocked → retrieve in context → continue/next text.
- **Long-term value/moat:** a private corpus of encounters, form/lemma/sense evidence, anchored errors, cross-text recurrence, and declining scaffolding.
- **Dependency:** linguistic resolver, content/artifacts, audio, append-only events, FSRS, grader; mentor helps but is not required.
- **Competitive archetype:** LingQ/Readlang plus Hebrew-specific morphology and integrated memory.
- **Build/operate cost:** medium-high build; moderate runtime with deterministic/local fallbacks; corpus curation and TTS are ongoing costs.
- **Success:** learners complete authentic fragments and later retrieve the same knowledge in a new context with fewer hints.
- **Kill criterion:** after a guided cohort, the flow does not beat translator+dictionary on time-to-comprehension or contextual delayed recall.

## User loop

`real text need → open/import → readable enriched fragment → evidence saved at the exact sentence → later contextual retrieval/adaptation → return for the next meaningful fragment`

## Data flywheel

Consumes text, resolver evidence, learner projections, consent and content metadata. Creates reading progress, taps, marks, anchored review events and hint usage. The moat is not raw text volume; it is consented longitudinal evidence linking form, lemma, sense, context, modality, delay and transfer. Do not collect unrelated personal text, passive scroll surveillance, raw prompts beyond TTL, or inferred sensitive topics.

## Surface boundary

- **Studio:** intentional transformation and editing of user-owned material; success ends with a trustworthy learning artifact and “Read now.” It must not become daily review, corpus browsing, or a mentor chat.
- **Reading Room:** uninterrupted consumption, discovery, tap help, lightweight marking, contextual due and next reading. Training remains short and dismissible; dashboards, bulk editing, provider controls and long explanations stay out of the reading flow.

## Failure mode and anti-goals

It fails as a highlighted translator, dictionary drawer, card factory, or enthusiast workbench. Gates: reading completion cannot fall as enrichment rises; exact morphology needs resolver provenance; due must not dominate fresh reading; no silent changes to word/memory state; all core reading remains useful offline/degraded.

## Fact / inference / proposal

**FACT:** This is the most mature differentiated loop. **INFERENCE:** it is the best acquisition wedge and evidence generator. **PROPOSAL:** market the outcome (“read real Hebrew and remember it”), not the internal name “Reading Intelligence.”

## Sources / assumptions / unresolved

Retention recon; Room continuity; Room UX audit; Ben-Yehuda strategy; morphology plans/audits; competitor audit. Assumes primary users already seek authentic reading. Unresolved: activation benchmark, comprehension lift, reading-flow interruption rate, contextual-transfer baseline.
