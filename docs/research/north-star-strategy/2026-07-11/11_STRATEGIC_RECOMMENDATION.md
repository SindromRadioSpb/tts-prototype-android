## North Star sentence

**LinguistPro helps independent Hebrew learners turn the real texts they want to understand into knowledge they can remember and use later, by connecting focused reading, timely practice, and guidance grounded in their own learning history.**

## Strategic formula

**Real-text wedge → evidence-grounded relationship → invisible learning platform.**

## Product identity and entry

Today LinguistPro is best described as a **real-text Hebrew learning system**, not yet a market-validated OS. The primary segment is the independent A2–B2 learner already reading or trying to read authentic Hebrew. The ten-second promise is: **“Read real Hebrew now—and remember what you learn from it.”**

Use a two-lane first entry: “Read a short curated text” (default) and “Add my text” (Studio). The first impression should occur in the Reading Room because it demonstrates the core job with less blank-canvas friction; urgent-message campaigns may deep-link directly to Studio.

## Surface roles

- **Studio:** deliberate creation/enrichment; ends with a trustworthy artifact and Read now.
- **Reading Room:** continuous reading/discovery with minimal contextual help and retrieval; it is the flagship daily-driver.
- **Mentor:** explains, recommends and continues evidence-backed loops; optional and contextual, with a full-screen Home for the plan—not a required chat.
- **Home/Today:** yes, but only a coordinating shell: one action, one reason, one progress proof, four destinations max. It must not replace Room/Studio or expose platform architecture.

## Dominant loop and metric

`Meaningful text → focused reading → contextual difficulty/evidence → one timed retrieval → success in a new context → next meaningful text.`

North Star Metric: **weekly learners with ≥1 independently gradeable knowledge transfer in a new context after ≥24 hours.** Supporting: fragment completion, delayed recall, hint dependency, reading continuation, CTA acceptance, W1/W4 return, false-grade/annul rate and cost per transfer.

## NOW / NEXT / LATER / NOT NOW

**NOW:** approve identity/segment/metric; simplify entry and Studio→Room handoff as a proposal before UI work; define transfer event/rubric; complete P8.6 and lifecycle/ops proof; instrument baseline; test Today vs direct Room.

**NEXT:** curated graded progression; reason-aware mentor actions with conservative construct confidence; notification activation experiment under one budget; improve cross-text recommendations and scaffolding fade after dependencies pass.

**LATER:** productive speaking/writing, personal curriculum engine, teacher/author tools, richer integrations and native mobile shell.

**NOT NOW:** generic AI chat, OS-first marketing, full beginner course, certification, social/community, broad teacher dashboard, automatic personal-text upload.

**STOP / MERGE / SIMPLIFY:** stop treating legacy roadmaps as current; merge duplicate counters/plans into Today projections; simplify dashboards to one CTA; subordinate standalone SRS/card language to the reading loop; keep Anki as interoperable companion, not competing memory truth; defer free-form output grading.

## 12-month strategic outcome

A new independent learner can understand the product in ten seconds, complete a real Hebrew fragment in the first session, and see one difficulty reappear later in a new context. Returning users experience one coherent loop across Room, Studio and optional mentor channels; scaffolding visibly declines; transfer and costs are measurable; public cloud/mentor use passes lifecycle, isolation and rollback gates. The product is known for converting authentic Hebrew into durable ability—not for the number of tools it contains.
