# 09 — Metrics and evaluation

**Status:** PROPOSAL · **Source commit:** `b426b1b7a91abcb4afb8fde7f0e34c042a9bc0d6` · **Date:** 2026-07-11

## North Star Metric candidates

| Candidate | Strength | Weakness / anti-gaming rule |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly learners with a **confirmed contextual transfer** | Closest to durable usable learning | Sparse/delayed; requires independent context and minimum interval |
| Reading-to-recall continuity weeks | Legible loop and frequency | Can reward drill without transfer |
| Knowledge units confirmed across 2+ contexts and 2 modalities | Strong evidence breadth | Complex identity/construct semantics |
| Meaningful learning sessions | Useful early proxy | Must require authentic input + saved evidence + later consequence |

**Recommended NSM:** **Weekly learners who confirm at least one knowledge transfer in a new context after a delay.** A confirmation requires: canonical item/construct identity; a source encounter; ≥24h delay; a different sentence/text or productive modality; deterministic/human-verifiable grading provenance; no hint beyond the declared threshold. Report denominator and eligibility so sparse users are not hidden.

## Supporting learning outcomes

Delayed recall at 1/7/30 days; contextual transfer; listening recognition; productive use where safely graded; hint/niqqud dependency; misconception recurrence; reading completion and text-complexity progression; abstention/calibration quality.

## Product health

Activation = authentic fragment completed + first anchored evidence + visible next step. Track time-to-first-readable-sentence; W1/W4 return; reading continuation; recommended CTA acceptance/completion; Studio→Room and Mentor→Room handoff; session quality; notification mute/not-today; cloud-consent conversion without coercion.

## Guardrails

Engagement without outcome lift; excessive hints; false grading/annul rate; LLM/TTS cost per confirmed transfer; notification fatigue; frustration/abandonment after intervention; privacy/security incident; cross-tenant access; UI destinations/steps growth; reading time displaced by drills; MNAR incorrectly written as grade.

## Evaluation design

1. Establish local-first baseline cohort before changing entry/Home.
2. Validate identity/transfer rubric on a human-reviewed gold sample; grader cannot evaluate itself.
3. Compare deterministic next-due control vs personalized mentor recommendation on CTA completion **and delayed transfer**.
4. Segment by level, material source and consent tier; report missingness explicitly.
5. Promote only if learning effect and cost/guardrails pass; owner live-verification is not evidence of effect.

## Facts / assumptions / unresolved / sources

**FACT:** event/projection/grader foundations exist; P10 outcome analytics is not built. **INFERENCE:** transfer is the best outcome construct but needs leading proxies during early cohorts. **PROPOSAL:** instrument only after metric definitions and privacy review. **ASSUMPTION:** enough eligible delayed encounters occur weekly to make the NSM actionable. **UNRESOLVED:** eligibility thresholds, human gold capacity, minimum sample, cost ceiling. Sources: retention/CLG recons, R10/R11/R17.
