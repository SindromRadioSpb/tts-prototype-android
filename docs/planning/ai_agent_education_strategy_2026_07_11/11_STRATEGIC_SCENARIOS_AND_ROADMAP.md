# 11 — Strategic scenarios and roadmap

**Status:** PROPOSAL · **Date:** 2026-07-11

| Scenario | Promise/moat | Platform and staffing | 12/24-month condition | Cost/risk/kill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 Conservative / Reading-led | understand real Hebrew now and retain it in context; Hebrew evidence + anchors + transfer | deterministic kernel, transfer/eval, bounded explainer/planner, curation | 12m: prove activation/CCT/tool replacement; 24m: expand only validated contexts | lowest; underuses agents; kill a bounded AI feature if no CCT/tool consolidation |
| S2 Balanced / Agent-assisted | one explainable next action across reading, content and evaluation | thin orchestrator, typed specialists, isolated backstage planes, model gateway | 12m only if S1 experiments pass; 24m selective curriculum/listening | medium/high; coordination/governance; kill specialists if <5pp gain or poor cost/CCT |
| S3 Agent-native | multimodal personal curriculum and background agents | full control/context/eval/durable platform, larger team | 12m DO NOT BUILD; reconsider after external validity and durable-workflow triggers | highest; feature supermarket/permission/cost; kill absent ≥1k MAU/3 long workflows |

## Recommendation

Fund **S1**. Treat S2 as individually gated experiments, not an architecture commitment. S3 is not the primary choice because the educational denominator, curriculum ontology, public governance and economics are unproven; more autonomy would magnify these gaps.

## Roadmap matrix

| Phase | Outcome | Epics | Dependency | Gate | Cost band | Risk/rollback | Evidence produced |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–30d | valid definitions and safe pilot boundary | CCT/hint/missingness; gold; provider/privacy decision; cost ledger design | owner NSM/privacy/cap | rubric agreement, A/A, policy review | low–M | docs/schemas only; no runtime change | baseline and preregistration |
| 31–60d | least-agentic shadow evidence | typed tools/policy/context/gateway design; shadow explainer/planner/content | phase 1 contracts | replay, isolation, locked-gold, no writes | M | retain current deterministic flow | quality/cost/calibration data |
| 61–90d | external-pilot-ready decision, not automatic launch | one experiment prototype; curation starter paths; ops drills | lifecycle/security gates | tenant/delete/restore/kill/load + owner sign-off | M | feature flags/off; deterministic core | pilot go/no-go packet |
| 3–6m | one causal learner experiment | assignment/exposure/probes; grounded explanation or planner RCT | enough eligible external users | ITT CCT SESOI + guardrails/cost | M–H | revert to control | first causal outcome |
| 6–12m | validated reading-led product | W1/W4, CCT, tool replacement/WTP; selective scaffold/next-text | successful first experiment | contribution margin and trust | high | stop weak capabilities | product/pedagogy/economic proof |
| 12–24m conditional | selective S2 expansion | listening, content agents, thin specialist, durable batch | ≥2 replicated outcomes, ≥3 long workflows or scale | each independent promotion gate | high | modular removal | scalable evidence, not agent count |

At 12 months product proof means target learners understand the promise, activate and replace a named tool; pedagogical proof means delayed independent CCT and lower hint dependency; technical proof means isolation/lifecycle/replay/degraded mode; economic proof means acceptable fully loaded cost/CCT and plausible contribution margin.
