# 08 — Pedagogical model and user control

**Status:** PROPOSAL · **Date:** 2026-07-11

## Pedagogical contract

**PROPOSAL:** optimize `delayed, novel, unassisted, construct-matched success`, not messages, immediate completion or FSRS stability alone. FSRS deterministically controls `when`; an evidence-grounded planner may propose `what/context/channel`; AI may explain or generate candidates; an independent grader/human confirms outcome.

Mastery is a vector over `construct × modality × evidence scope`, including contextual reading recognition, decontextualized receptive meaning, listening, form recall, dictation and constrained production. Reading tap, multiple choice or revealed success cannot promote productive mastery. Speaking/free-writing remain **UNKNOWN** until independently calibrated.

## Transfer and scaffolding

A confirmed transfer needs a novel target context, declared delay, no answer leakage/hints before first response, same versioned construct/sense and independent scoring. Same-item recall is retention evidence, not transfer.

Scaffolding ladder: user default → smallest useful hint → reveal → supported completion → later unsupported probe → optional fade recommendation. Log aid availability, shown/requested/automatic, level, latency and completion. Contingent fade is a suggestion with preview and instant undo; it never silently removes accessibility support.

## Missingness and misconception

Separate `explicit_dont_know` (gradable lapse policy) from `not_attempted`, technical failure, unsupported input, modality decline and abandonment. Analytics retains missing opportunities in the ITT denominator and runs sensitivity analyses.

A misconception requires repeated diagnostic signature across at least 2–3 independent contexts/items, ambiguity/hints excluded, confidence decay and learner correction. One error is an observation, never a mental-state claim.

## Cold start and accessibility

Cold start uses self-declared level/goals, a curated 90-second text and minimal deterministic diagnostic; no confident personal curriculum. Core works without account, cloud, model or network. Accessibility includes keyboard/screen-reader/RTL/reflow/contrast/reduced-motion/target-size checks, adjustable typography, transcripts and modality substitution. User preference is authoritative over inferred adaptation.

Evidence: retrieval transfer is conditional ([Butler review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29733621/)); computer scaffolding shows positive average effects but limited evidence that fading itself dominates ([meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5347356/)); receptive and productive retrieval require separate claims.
