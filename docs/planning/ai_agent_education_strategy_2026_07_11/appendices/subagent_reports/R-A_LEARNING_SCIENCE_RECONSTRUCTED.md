## Mandate and findings

Retrieval, transfer, scaffolding, SLA, mastery, productive/receptive evidence, misconceptions and measurement validity.

- **BLOCKER:** ordinary same-item recall/FSRS stability is not contextual transfer. Confirmation needs a versioned construct/sense, novel uncontaminated target, delay, unassisted first attempt and an independent rubric.
- **MAJOR:** educational state is `construct × modality × evidence scope`; reading recognition, reveal and multiple choice cannot promote productive mastery.
- **MAJOR:** adaptive scaffolding is previewable and undoable. Log support available/shown/requested, hint level and later unsupported result. Assisted success cannot stand alone as unassisted mastery.
- **MAJOR:** distinguish explicit “don’t know” from not attempted, technical failure, abandonment and declined modality. Missingness is potentially MNAR.
- **MAJOR:** one error is an observation, not a misconception. Require repeated diagnostic signatures across 2–3 independent contexts, ambiguity exclusion, confidence decay and learner correction.
- **PROPOSAL:** deterministic FSRS owns `when`; planner may propose context/channel; LLM may explain; independent delayed probe owns outcome.

Sources recovered: [Pan & Rickard](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29733621/), [Butler 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20804289/), [scaffolding meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5347356/), retention recon and docs `08`/`09`.

Falsifiable tests: contextual retrieval vs decontextualized card; fixed/manual vs adaptive scaffold; channel-gap targeting vs generic due; grounded explanation vs local facts. Outcomes are delayed unsupported transfer, with reading completion, hints and missingness as guardrails.

Rejected: engagement as learning; unified mastery; self-grading; speaking/writing certification. Gap: no systematic Hebrew-specific SLA review; this is a reconstruction from findings, not the original report.
