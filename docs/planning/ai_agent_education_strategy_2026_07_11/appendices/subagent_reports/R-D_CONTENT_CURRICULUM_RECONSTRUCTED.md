## Findings

- **FACT:** a 26K+ discovery corpus is not a curriculum; the ready subset is smaller and historically/literarily skewed. `ez` is a derived proxy, not CEFR or comprehensibility.
- **BLOCKER:** no validated Hebrew construct/prerequisite graph or work-level gold for comprehensibility, register, cultural sensitivity, translation and exercise quality.
- **PROPOSAL:** create versioned work-learning cards and three curator-owned starter paths. Path selection is constraint satisfaction over learner choice, coverage, register/period, ambiguity, modalities and constructs—not one opaque score.
- **PROPOSAL:** pipeline is candidate generation → deterministic identity/licence/schema/morphology/uniqueness checks → independent critic → human immutable-hash approval → deterministic canary publish/rollback.
- **MAJOR:** originals and adaptations remain separate, visible and reversible. LLM never overwrites morphology or rights truth.

Tests: 60–100 stratified works with blinded A2/B1/B2 reviewers; curated vs existing rail vs AI path; 300 cloze/distractor candidates for alternative-correct answers; simplification followed by authentic delayed transfer; editor time/error; release rollback.

Rejected: auto-CEFR, autonomous curriculum graph, semantic auto-publishing, generator self-grade and silent text replacement. Sources: corpus/editorial/build paths, [CEFR companion volume](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-companion-volume-and-its-language-versions), Extensive Reading Foundation guidance. Tension: automation breadth is limited by R-F governance and R-G reviewer cost.
