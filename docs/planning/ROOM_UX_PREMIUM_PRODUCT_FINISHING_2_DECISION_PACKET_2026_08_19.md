## Executive decision

Recommend `ACCESSIBILITY_NECESSITY_ONLY`.

VF0–VF5 remains closed and `B_EDITORIAL_CALM` remains the accepted visual thesis. Current production is compositionally coherent enough that neither a premium portfolio nor a design-system/theme rewrite is justified. One measurable accessibility family crosses the successor gate: four reachable light-mode secondary/informational text selector groups in Library/L0 and Studio measure `4.34:1`, `4.37:1`, `2.56:1` and `2.36:1`, below the `4.5:1` normal-text contract.

The proposed first and only slice is a selector-local reassignment to existing semantic secondary-text roles. It changes no hierarchy, geometry, copy, behavior, data or truth ownership. Shared-table localized names and Studio form-label findings remain explicit backlog.
