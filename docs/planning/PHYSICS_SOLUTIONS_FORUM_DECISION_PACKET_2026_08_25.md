## Executive decision

Recommend D — HYBRID_STAGED_TASK_BOUND_REGISTRY_FIRST.

The smallest useful product is not a forum. It is an owner-curated registry of typed external solutions/resources bound to an immutable physics task edition, visible anonymously after approval. It helps one learner immediately and creates clean evidence for whether native solution text, Q&A and attachments are needed.

Do not open community writes on the existing OWNER/MEMBER identity. Do not show empty threads. Do not fetch/preview arbitrary URLs. Do not accept binaries. Do not reuse publication, group, notes, reading lists, review_log, nudge or Telegram truth.

Recommended pilot needs a new additive metadata schema if implementation is later approved. This packet proposes its contract but creates/executes no migration.

## One-writer, transaction and idempotency rules

- One new forum repository owns all pilot mutations.
- publicationRepo is read-only for anchor validation; no forum FK mutation inside publication writer.
- BEGIN IMMEDIATE transaction covers bounded canonical rows, required moderation/review event and idempotent receipt.
- Request key namespace is principal + operation + key; same request hash returns receipt, different hash is 409.
- URL/provider calls, cache warm and telemetry occur outside canonical transaction.
- Expected revision prevents lost update.
- Public projection becomes visible only after anchor, URL, rights and moderation validation; cache invalidation is part of visible outcome or fails closed.

## Native/Q&A/attachment GO thresholds

Native text: 8 weeks, at least 25 distinct verified requesters/writers, 100 task-bound resource opens/week, 20 approved external resources, and either over 10% monthly access failure or material owner authoring friction; identity/reviewer/moderator/export/delete green.

Q&A: 8 weeks, at least 30 verified askers, 15 responders, 40 unresolved task-bound questions/month, duplicate search resolves under half, moderator SLO capacity proven four weeks, minors/PII policy resolved.

Attachments: at least 10% approved attempts blocked without a binary plus complete scanning/quarantine/quota/legal/object-backup/restore/cost proof.

Failure to meet a threshold means continue registry/external platform or NO_GO; it does not authorize lowering safety controls.
